Shortee Research publishes analysis of the microdrama industry: how big it is, where it is growing, and how its audiences watch. The research is led by Nelson Moses Lassiter.

## Nelson Moses Lassiter, Founder & CEO

Emmy-nominated film and TV executive and founder of Shortee. He spent five years in Original Series Production at **Netflix**, served as a Creative Executive at **Tubi** developing and producing 15+ films, and later worked as a Creative Executive at **DramaBox**, including AI Content Innovation. One of the few executives to have worked inside premium streaming, AVOD, and global vertical drama, he brings the exact experience Shortee was built on.

## How the research works

Every published figure names its source and links to where it was read. The analysis and calculations are Shortee Research's own; the underlying data belongs to the firms that publish it. The full rules are on the [methodology]({{ '/methodology/' | relative_url }}) page, and errors are recorded on the [corrections]({{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}) page.
