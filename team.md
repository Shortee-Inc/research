## How an article is made

1. **The topic comes from a real search about the industry.** Every article answers a phrase people actually search about microdramas and vertical series, in Google or on social platforms. Searches for Shortee itself are never used as a topic. The phrase is the article's title and web address.
2. **It is written only from facts we can source.** Facts about Shortee come from one checked record, each with a link to where it can be verified. Facts about other apps link to a public source. A number that is not in that record is refused before anyone reads the draft.
3. **Checked before it goes out.** Drafts are prepared with software from those facts. A draft that fails any check above is never published. Articles that compare Shortee with other apps are published only after Shortee's founder has read and approved them.
4. **Errors are corrected in public** on the [corrections]({{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}) page.
