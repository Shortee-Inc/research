## How an article is made

1. **The topic comes from a real search.** Every article answers a phrase people actually searched: in Google (from our Search Console data) or on social platforms. The phrase is the article's title and web address.
2. **It is written only from facts we can source.** Facts about Shortee come from one checked record, each with a link to where it can be verified. Facts about other apps link to a public source. A number that is not in that record is refused before anyone reads the draft.
3. **A person approves it.** Drafts are prepared with software from those facts and are published only after Shortee's founder has read and approved each one.
4. **Errors are corrected in public** on the [corrections]({{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}) page.

The full rules are on the [methodology]({{ '/methodology/' | relative_url }}) page.
